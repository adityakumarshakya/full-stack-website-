Dr. Émile Parfait Simb

Chairman of SIMB GROUP
Dunster Business School — Zug, Switzerland

Awarded by Philippe Thévenot, President of Dunster Business School — Ceremony, New Delhi, May 16, 2026.

"You join a very select circle of individuals whose achievements have profoundly shaped their field and society. Your commitment to Blockchain Technology, Web3 and cryptocurrencies continues to inspire our community and illustrates the significant difference you bring to the lives of others — this is the very essence of excellence."
— Philippe Thévenot, Président, Dunster Business School (January 2026)

Born on March 1, 1983, Dr. Émile Parfait Simb is a Cameroonian businessman, teacher, author and international consultant recognized as one of the African pioneers of blockchain.

Founder of Simb Group and Global Investment Trading (GIT), he has been working for over a decade to democratize digital investment and promote a sovereign Africa.

Inspiring the Next Generation

Dr. Émile Parfait Simb is the author of two major works aimed at inspiring African youth. His latest book, "From Part-Time Teacher to Crypto-Millionaire", was dedicated in April 2021 in the Central African Republic by President Faustin-Archange Touadéra himself.

Through his books, he teaches resilience, courage, discipline and faith in Africa — guiding a new generation of entrepreneurs, investors and builders.

Making Change Liberia

Strategic Partner — Social Impact & Community Development
Liberia, West Africa  ·  Founded 2013

Mission: To empower communities through sustainable development, education, health, and economic opportunity.

"To mobilize people and resources towards a common cause and a shared outlook for a better future."
— Vision, Making Change Liberia

Making Change Liberia is a non-governmental and social enterprise organization dedicated to creating long-term, sustainable change in Liberia through community-driven development. With a strong focus on agriculture, education, health and economic empowerment, the organization has grown from a small grassroots initiative into a respected leader in social impact — bridging local communities with international partners.

Programs & Initiatives

Education programs for children and youth (school construction, teacher training, menstrual hygiene support for girls), health initiatives (vaccination drives, maternal and child health, community health education), economic empowerment through microfinance, vocational training and entrepreneurship support, and disaster response during crises like Ebola and COVID-19.

Motto

"Get inspired and be a change."

Emmanuel Campbell Sr.

Liberia Partner — IT & Digital Solutions
Capitol Bye-Pass, Monrovia, Liberia

Emmanuel Campbell Sr. is an information technology professional based in Monrovia, Liberia, with a strong focus on helping organizations modernize their operations through practical, dependable digital solutions.

His work spans web development, systems administration, network setup and technical support — pairing hands-on engineering with a clear understanding of the challenges local businesses, NGOs and public-sector teams face when adopting new technology.

As Orinnovative's partner in Liberia, Emmanuel leads client engagements across website development, custom software, IT infrastructure and digital transformation projects, ensuring solutions are designed for the realities of the West African market while meeting international quality standards.

He is known for translating complex technical requirements into simple, workable plans, and for building long-term relationships with clients who need a trusted local point of contact for their IT needs.
